The files src/krylov_open_quantum/data/neel_51ion_imbalance.csv and
src/krylov_open_quantum/data/neel_51ion_site_imbalance.csv are derived
summaries of the experimental Neel-state measurements deposited by Manoj Kumar
Joshi, Florian Kranzl, Johannes Franke, Roos Roos, and Rainer Blatt in
"Bounded-Error Quantum Simulation via Hamiltonian and Lindbladian Learning,"
Zenodo, version 1, 2025, DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17750419.

The source dataset is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International (CC BY 4.0). The first summary reports, at each of 11 times, the
mean and standard error of the staggered spin imbalance across 20 batches, 50
shots per batch, and 51 ions. The second reports the corresponding mean and
standard error separately for every ion and time (561 ion-time records). Both
can be regenerated from the archive file
DataHL51IonmeasType14Base3.mat by decoding each decimal shot into 51 bits,
mapping bits 0 and 1 to spin values +1 and -1, and correlating the spin string
with the initial alternating Neel pattern. The sign convention is chosen so
the initial aggregate imbalance is positive.
